# Revercue SEO, AEO and GEO strategy (research date: 25 November 2026)

## Status

- **Published:** three Day 1 articles, plus two long-form articles (below).
- **Queued:** thirteen further articles, one per opportunity listed in the table. Each is written only after its research is complete, so none is published with unverified claims.
- **Not verified:** search volume, keyword difficulty, and whether any AI assistant recommends Revercue. The tools available for this work return search results and page text. They do not return search volume, difficulty scores, or AI answer data, so those fields are left empty rather than estimated.

## Research basis

Research used web search results only. The evidence for each opportunity is the set of pages that rank for it, the questions that competitor FAQ pages answer, and the vendor pages checked on 24 November 2026. Several sources are competitors' own blogs. Those are treated as claims to check, not as facts. The vendor prices used in articles were checked against each vendor's own pricing page, or against the source named in the article.

## SEO strategy

Target buyer-intent phrases one cluster at a time. Each article answers one buyer question in full, links to the matching feature page, and states its prices with a date and a source. The differentiator is the verified pricing and the honest scope. Next steps are to add internal links from the feature and pricing pages to the articles, and to submit the sitemap to search engines once Search Console is set up.

## AEO strategy

Answer each question in the first sentence, then give the detail. Use question-style headings. Keep FAQ sections on every article. Next steps are to add FAQPage structured data once the blog template can render it safely, and to check each article against the question it targets before publishing.

## GEO strategy

Publish facts that a reader or an AI system can cite: prices with dates and sources, a plain description of what each tool is built around, and a clear statement of what the product does not do. Get Revercue listed on third-party sites where comparison queries are answered, following each site's rules. Monitor AI answers for the target queries by hand each month. Do not claim an AI platform recommends Revercue unless that has been tested and recorded.

## How the three support each other

SEO brings readers to the pages. AEO makes the answers easy to quote. GEO supplies the facts that comparison answers draw on. All three share the same verified facts, so a correction made in one place is made in all three.

## Thirty-day roadmap

- **Week 1:** Publish the Day 1 articles and the two long-form articles. Confirm each one renders correctly on mobile.
- **Week 2:** Add internal links from the feature and pricing pages. List Revercue on G2 and Product Hunt, following each site's rules, and only after the approval you have set.
- **Weeks 3 and 4:** Write the queued articles that have verified evidence, starting with #5 and #10. Recheck every vendor price against its page before publishing.

## Measurement plan

Do not record baselines until the tools are connected.

- **SEO:** indexed pages, impressions, clicks and rankings for the target queries, from Search Console.
- **AEO:** whether each targeted question is answered on the page, and any referral traffic from answer engines where it can be measured.
- **GEO:** manual checks of AI answers for the target queries each month, and third-party mentions.
- **Business:** qualified visitors, sign-ups, offer engagement and paid conversions, from analytics and the product.
