# REVERCUE — DASHBOARD BLUEPRINT
# File: dashboard.txt
# The authenticated dashboard. The shell, the Overview, the list screens, the settings, the notifications.
# The rules: no "AI", no "MVP", no "Payoneer", Teams in beta. The gold rule. The six states. The dark/light/system mode.

## THE SHELL
### The top bar (AppHeader)
Sticky, 64px, bg var(--bg-base), 1px bottom border.
Left: the logo. Center: the 7 nav (Dashboard, Proposals, Contracts, Forms, Invoices, CRM, Settings). Active: gold underline. Right: the NotificationBell (the unread badge, piggybacked) + the avatar (Profile, Settings, Log out).
Mobile (<768px): the logo left, the bell right, a hamburger.

### The sidebar (AppSidebar)
Contextual: Dashboard (hidden), Proposals (the filters + Create), Contracts, Forms, Invoices (the filters), CRM (the filters + Add client), Settings (the 9 sections).
240px, bg var(--bg-base), 1px right border. Active: 2px gold left border.
Mobile: a bottom sheet.

### The notification bell
The unread count from the page response (the dashboard summary, the list responses). No poll. Click: the dropdown (380px, the last 10). Footer: View all -> /notifications.

## THE OVERVIEW (/dashboard)
1. The FormSubmit banner (if not activated): "Activate FormSubmit to receive emails. We sent an activation email to [email]. Click the link. One-time step." [I clicked] [x].
2. The greeting (serif 32px): "Good morning, [Name]." The date (caption).
3. The stat strip (4 cards): Active documents, Clients, Follow-ups due (red if >0), Revenue this month. Each card links.
4. The pending line: "Pending payments: $4,200 (3 invoices)." The overdue: red. Links to /invoices?filter=pending.
5. The suggested actions (Growth basic, Scale advanced): a card with the list (max 5). "Do it" (gold text), "Dismiss" (ghost). Free/Starter: hidden.
6. The create row: "Create proposal" (gold), "Create contract", "Create invoice", "Create form" (ghost).
7. The recent activity: the last 10. Empty: "Nothing yet."
8. The empty state (first login): "Welcome to Revercue. You don't have any documents yet." + "Create your first proposal" (gold) + "Read the Guides."

## THE 4 LIST SCREENS
Each: the header (the title + the Create gold), the filter bar (the pills), the search, the table (the Table component), the empty state, the loading (skeleton), the error (retry).
- Proposals: Title, Client, Status, Last activity, Actions (Duplicate, Archive, Delete).
- Contracts: Title, Client, Status, After approval, Last activity.
- Forms: Title, Client, Status, Submissions, Last activity.
- Invoices: Invoice #, Client, Status, Total, Due date, Last activity. The filter: All / Drafts / Published / Paid / Overdue / Pending.

## THE CRM LIST (/crm)
The header: "Clients" + "+ Add client" (gold) + "Export CSV".
The filter: All / Active / Archived / Not interested. The search. The sort.
The table: Name, Email, Channel, Last message, Documents, Latest status, Interest (Scale), Actions.
The Add client modal: name, email, company, phone, channel.
The empty state: "No clients yet. Create a proposal, contract, invoice, or form — the client is added automatically. Or add one manually."

## THE CLIENT DETAIL (/crm/:id)
The header: the name, the email (mono), the company, the channel, the interest badge (Scale), the "Create proposal/contract/invoice/form" buttons.
The communication log (the main body, the timeline): the icon, the direction, the channel, the message, the estimated time, the linked document, the next step, the follow-up date, the relative time, the Mark done.
The documents (grouped: Proposals, Contracts, Invoices, Forms).
The notes (Free 3, Starter 10, Growth+ unlimited). The limit message.
The follow-ups (the open, the Mark done).
The interest breakdown (Scale): the modal (the score, the signals, the arithmetic).
The revenue tab (Scale): the earned, the spent, the profit, the expense list, the price suggestion.
The writing area (the modal): the client pre-selected, the message, the helper (if available), the "Copy message" (Revercue does not send), the "Save to log."

## THE SETTINGS (/settings)
The left nav: Profile, Plan & Billing, Security, Notifications, Saved FAQs, Saved Packages, Revision Prices, What Revercue Knows, Appearance, Delete Account.
Each section: a card + "Save changes" (gold, disabled until a change) + "Cancel."
- Profile: name, email (read-only), business, website, Fiverr, services, brand colours, logo.
- Plan & Billing: the current plan, the usage bars (red at 90%), the waitlist status (not the payment history), the upgrade (the waitlist modal).
- Security: the password change (the current, the new, the confirm), the 2FA toggle, the sessions list, the log out all.
- Notifications: the email notifications (Growth/Scale), the per-event toggles, the newsletter, the FormSubmit status card.
- Saved FAQs (Growth/Scale): the list, the Add FAQ, the Keep it (the 10-day warning).
- Saved Packages (Growth/Scale): the list, the Add package, the Keep it.
- Revision Prices (Scale/Teams): the category + price list.
- What Revercue Knows (Scale/Teams): the read-only transparency page.
- Appearance: the [Dark] [Light] [System] segmented control. The current mode. The change saves to localStorage.
- Delete Account: the danger card, the type-your-email confirmation, the 5-day grace, the cancel.

## THE NOTIFICATIONS PAGE (/notifications)
The header: "Notifications" + "Mark all as read." The filter: All / Unread / Client activity / System. The list (25/page): the icon, the description, the relative time, the link, the dismiss. The empty: "No notifications yet."

## THE DESIGN RULES
The gold rule: one gold per screen (the Create, the Save changes).
The six states: every interactive element.
The empty state: every list.
The loading: the skeleton.
The error: the retry.
The dark/light/system mode: the tokens.
The client record: the thread's home. The communication log is the main body.
The settings: the appearance section (the dark/light/system).
The FormSubmit copy: exact.
No "AI", no "MVP", no "Payoneer", Teams in beta.
