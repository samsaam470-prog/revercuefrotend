## The tagline

## The design system (the reference: design.txt)
The dark base (#0A0A0A), the gold (#C9A961), the serif headlines (Instrument Serif), the sans body (Inter), the mono for copyables (JetBrains Mono). The six states on every interactive element. The gold rule: one gold button per screen.

## The footer (the four buttons, not an AI chat)
The footer has four buttons: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini. Each opens the external tool with a pre-filled prompt about Revercue. The answer comes from the external tool. Revercue does not host it.
The prompt: "Explain Revercue: a freelancer tool for the thread from proposal to contract to invoice to CRM. The tagline is 'Your work should speak, not you.' The thesis is 'a thread, not disconnected workflows.' The Free plan is available now. The paid plans (Starter, Growth, Scale) are invite-only in the fresh state — join the waitlist. Teams is in beta — join the beta. The product does not use AI; it uses patterns. Answer in 2–3 sentences."

## The rules (every page)
- No "AI", no "local model", no "browser model". The replacement: "the helper", "the rewrite", "the suggestion", "the review".
- No "MVP", no "v2", no "v3". The replacement: "available now", "invite-only, join the waitlist", "in beta, join the beta".
- No "Payoneer", no "send the receipt". The replacement: "the paid plans are invite-only. Join the waitlist. The team will reach out when a spot opens."
- Teams is in beta. The copy: "Teams is in beta. Join the beta."
- The footer: the four external AI buttons (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini) with the pre-filled prompt. Not an AI chat.
- The FAQ count: 100. The Home page: "Show all 100."
- The visuals: the `<CodeVisual>` components, not screenshots.
- The tagline: "Your work should speak, not you."
- The thesis: "A thread, not disconnected workflows."
