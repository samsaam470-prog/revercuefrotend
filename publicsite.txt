# REVERCUE — PUBLIC SITE BLUEPRINT
# File: publicsite.txt
# Purpose: The complete specification for the Revercue public marketing site.
# Audience: The AI or developer building the frontend. Read this file first.
# Do not deviate from the palette, typography, or layout contracts.
# Every screen must answer: who is the user, what are they doing in 10s, what is the smallest screen.

# ============================================================
# PART 0 — THE PRODUCT (what the public site sells)
# ============================================================

Revercue is a SaaS tool for freelancers and agencies.
Thesis: "A thread, not disconnected workflows."
The client work is one thread: proposal -> contract -> requirement form -> invoice -> client record.
The documents are interactive links, not PDFs.
The client opens a link, reads, answers embedded questions, asks questions, selects a package.
The freelancer sees the activity (opened, time spent, clicks, answers).
The client record builds itself.
The product does not use AI. It uses patterns (keyword matching, frequency counting).
The plans: Free, Starter, Growth ($29), Scale ($49), Teams ($99 base, MVP 2.0).

## Typography
Display: Serif (Instrument Serif or similar), 40-48px, weight 400. Public hero headlines only.
Headline: Serif, 28-32px, weight 400. Page titles, section headers.
Title: Sans (Inter or similar), 18-20px, weight 600. Card titles.
Body: Sans, 15px, weight 400. Paragraphs, descriptions.
Label: Sans, 13px, weight 500. Form labels, table headers, nav.
Caption: Sans, 12px, weight 400. Timestamps, helper text.
Mono: JetBrains Mono, 13px, weight 400. Links, reference numbers, copyable values.

Rule: serif for headlines only. Sans for everything else. Mono for copyables. No fourth font.

## Spacing
4px base scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96.
Card padding: 24px desktop, 16px mobile.
Section gap: 32px.
Page padding: 48px desktop, 24px mobile.

## Radius
Cards: 8px.
Modals: 12px.
Buttons: 6px.
Inputs: 6px.
Badges/tags: 999px.

## States (every interactive element must define all six)
Default: as specified.
Hover: background var(--bg-elevated), border var(--border-strong).
Focus: 2px solid var(--gold) ring, offset 2px.
Active: background var(--bg-card), 2px gold left border.
Disabled: 50% opacity, cursor not-allowed.
Loading: 16px spinner replaces label, button width fixed.
Error: 1px solid var(--red) border, error text below in caption red.

Rule: no interactive element ships without all six states.

## The gold rule
Gold is for ONE thing per screen: the primary action.
If two gold buttons appear, one is wrong. The secondary is a ghost button (transparent, 1px border, white text).

## Pages 12-14 — Privacy, Terms, Security
Shared legal layout:
  Left column: sticky table of contents (label size, current section gold). Hidden on mobile (a "Contents" collapsible at top).

  No Start Free band. Footer only.

Privacy sections:
  1. What we collect (account, product, tracking, payment — the exact list from "What Revercue knows about me").
  2. How we use it (the product, support, billing, suggestions — no sale, no advertising).
  3. Who we share it with (Cloudflare, Backblaze B2, Turso, FormSubmit, Payoneer — "store but do not read").
  4. Retention (document by plan, 5-day tracking, 90-day notifications, 3-month saved FAQs/packages, 5-day account deletion).
  5. Your rights (export, delete, correct, contact email, GDPR/CCPA line).
  6. Contact.

Terms sections:
  1. The service (what Revercue is, what it is not — not a law firm, not an accountant, not a payment processor).
  2. Your account (one person, accuracy, password security).
  3. Acceptable use (no illegal content, no spam, no API abuse, no reselling).
  4. Payment (the plans, Payoneer manual, no refunds MVP 1.0, downgrade rules).
  5. Termination (user can delete, Revercue can suspend for abuse, data handling).
  6. Liability (standard limitation, "as is").
  7. Governing law.

Security sections (a trust page, not a legal page):
  1. Encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest, bcrypt for passwords, Argon2id for document passwords).
  2. Storage (private B2 bucket, Worker proxy, no direct storage URLs, unguessable public IDs, noindex).
  3. Retention (5-day tracking, 90-day notifications, PDF-before-deletion).
  4. Access (the team sees account data, not content; the exception stated honestly).
  5. The third parties (the same list, "store but do not read").
  6. What we do not do (no AI analysis of content, no cross-site tracking, no data sale).

# ============================================================
# PART 6 — THE FAILURE MODES TO AVOID
# ============================================================

No generic forms. Every field has a label, a helper line, an error line, and six states. The question and its options live in one fieldset, not as siblings with no container.
No stock photos. Real screenshots only.
No second font. Serif headlines, sans body, mono for copyables. Three, no more.
No gold on two buttons per screen.
No public page without a CTA (except the legal pages — a CTA on a privacy page is tone-deaf).
No screen without the empty state defined.
No fake testimonials. If they do not exist, omit the section.
No AI in the product. Patterns are enough. The site must not claim AI.
