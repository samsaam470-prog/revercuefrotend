# File: compare.txt
# The /compare page. A factual comparison. No competitor bashing.

## The alternatives
1. Revercue — the thread: proposal, contract, form, invoice, CRM.
2. PDF + email — the traditional way.
3. Notion — the all-purpose workspace.

## The comparison table
| The job | Revercue | PDF + email | Notion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Interactive link: packages, FAQs, embedded questions, the client selects a package. | A static PDF. The client replies by email. | A page. The client reads it. No package selection. |
| Contract | Approve, Decline, or I Have Questions. The after-approval chain. | A PDF. The client signs and emails it back. | A page. The client reads it. No approve/decline. |
| Requirement form | Adaptive form with branching. The Prompt Creator on submit. | A Word doc or a Google Form. Manual. | A database or a page. Manual. |
| Invoice | Pay by link or account details + proof upload. The confirm payment. | A PDF. The client pays by bank transfer and emails the receipt. | A page. No payment. |
| Client record | Builds itself from the documents. The communication log, the notes, the follow-ups, the interest level (Scale). | None. The client is a name in the inbox. | A database. Manual. The client is a row. |
| Tracking | Opened, time spent, clicks, answers. By plan. | None. You do not know if the email was opened. | None. You do not know if the page was read. |
| Follow-ups | The dashboard shows what is due. The suggestion engine fires when a proposal is not answered. | Your memory. | A reminder. Manual. |
| Suggestions | The pattern engine: the follow-up, the document suggestion, the interest flag, the price suggestion. | None. | None. |
| Price | Free (available now). The paid plans are invite-only (join the waitlist). Teams is in beta (join the beta). | Free. | Free tier, then paid. |
| The design | The dark base, the gold, the serif headlines, the mono for copyables. The code-made visuals. | The PDF template. | The Notion template. |

## What Revercue is not
Revercue is not a CRM replacement. It is not a project manager. It is not an accounting tool. It is the thread: the proposal, the contract, the form, the invoice, and the client record, in one place. If you already have a CRM, Revercue can sit next to it. If you already have an accounting tool, Revercue can sit next to it. The thread is the gap; the other tools are the rest.
