# REVERCUE — CONTRACT BUILDER BLUEPRINT
# File: contractbuilder.txt
# Purpose: The contract builder. The requirement form, the template selection, the canvas, the after-approval chain, the client actions.
# Audience: The AI building the frontend. Read design.txt, api.txt, and proposalbuilder.txt first.
# The contract builder shares the canvas, the block library, the properties panel, the auto-save, the preview, and the share modal with the proposal builder. This file specifies the DIFFERENCES.

# ============================================================
# PART 1 — THE FLOW
# ============================================================

Stage 1: The requirement form (/contracts/new). The contract-specific fields.
Stage 2: The template selection (/contracts/:id/edit?step=template). The same template grid, filtered to contract templates.
Stage 3: The canvas (/contracts/:id/edit). The same three panels, with the contract-specific properties.

# ============================================================
# PART 2 — STAGE 1: THE REQUIREMENT FORM (the differences)
# ============================================================

The same layout as the proposal requirement form (centered card, header, footer). The fields differ:

1. Client (required). Same segmented control (Existing | New).
2. Which proposal is this contract for? (optional). A dropdown of the client's accepted/approved proposals. If selected, the scope, the pricing, and the timeline pre-fill from the proposal.
3. Scope of work (required). A textarea. Pre-filled from the linked proposal if selected.
4. Payment terms (required). A structured input: the amount, the schedule (e.g., "50% upfront, 50% on delivery"), the currency.
5. Revision policy (required). A textarea (e.g., "2 rounds of revisions included. Additional rounds at $50/hour.").
6. Cancellation policy (required). A textarea.
7. Which sections do you want? (required, at least one). A checkbox group: Parties, Scope, Payment, Timeline, Revisions, Cancellation, Liability, Signatures. (NO FAQs, NO Embedded Questions.)
8. After approval, what next? (required). A dropdown: "Show the requirement form" | "Show the invoice" | "Download PDF only". Helper: "This is what the client sees after they approve. The form/invoice options are available on Growth and Scale. On Free and Starter, the client sees the PDF only."
   If the user is on Free/Starter and selects form/invoice: a note "This option is available on Growth and Scale. On your plan, the client will see the PDF."

The footer: "Continue to builder" (gold, disabled until client, scope, payment terms, revision policy, cancellation policy, at least one section, after-approval).
"Cancel" (ghost).

The submit: POST /api/contracts/draft with { client_id, linked_proposal_id?, title, scope, payment_terms, timeline, revisions, cancellation, after_approval, currency }.

# ============================================================
# PART 3 — STAGE 2: THE TEMPLATE SELECTION (the differences)
# ============================================================

The same grid as the proposal builder, but filtered to contract templates (the templates with type = "contract").
The "Suggested" badge matches the user's services.
The "Start from blank" card is available.
The upload modal: the type is pre-set to "Contract".

# ============================================================
# PART 4 — STAGE 3: THE CANVAS (the differences)
# ============================================================

The same three panels. The differences:

## The block library (narrower)
The contract uses: heading, paragraph, list, divider, two-column, timeline, signature, "I Have Questions" block.
It does NOT have: FAQ list, embedded question, file upload, packages, pricing table (the payment terms are a paragraph/list, not a pricing table).
The signature block: a block with two signature lines (Freelancer, Client) and a date. On the client view, the client's signature line shows "Approve" / "Decline" (the contract's actions), not a drawn signature.

## The properties panel
The global properties (brand colours, font, page) are the same.
The "After approval" dropdown is in the document properties (a "Contract" tab): "Show the requirement form" | "Show the invoice" | "Download PDF only". The freelancer can change it here (it pre-filled from the requirement form).
The document-level "I Have Questions" toggle: on/off. If on, the client sees the "I Have Questions" button at the bottom of the contract.

## The canvas content
The sections: Parties, Scope, Payment, Timeline, Revisions, Cancellation, Liability, Signatures.
The "Signatures" section at the bottom: the two signature lines + the date. On the client view, the client's side has the Approve / Decline / I Have Questions buttons.

## The preview
The same live preview. The client's view shows:
  The contract title, "Prepared for [Client]", the reference number.
  The sections.
  The three actions at the bottom: "Approve" (gold), "Decline" (ghost, red border), "I Have Questions" (ghost, if the toggle is on).
  The Approve modal: "By approving, you confirm you have reviewed this contract. This is not a legally binding e-signature." Confirm.
  The Decline modal: "Are you sure?" + an optional reason. Confirm.
  The I Have Questions: a text box. Submit. The question goes to the freelancer's dashboard.

## The share modal
The same as the proposal, with the message pre-filled: "Hi [Client], here is the contract for [project]. Review it and let me know if you have any questions."
The line: "Revercue does not send the message. You send it from your own account."
If password-protected: the password (copyable) + "Send the password separately."

## The create (publish)
Same as the proposal. After publish, the client's view has the three actions.

## The after-approval chain (the client side)
When the client approves:
  If after_approval = "form": the client sees the requirement form (the freelancer's form, if one exists for this client) or a message "The freelancer will send you a form next."
  If after_approval = "invoice": the client sees the invoice (the freelancer's invoice, if one exists) or a message "The freelancer will send you an invoice next."
  If after_approval = "pdf_only": the client sees "Approved. Thank you." + a "Download PDF" button.
  On Free/Starter: always the PDF only (the form/invoice chain is Growth/Scale).

# ============================================================
# PART 5 — THE CLIENT-FACING CONTRACT VIEW (/c/:publicId)
# ============================================================

The same layout as the public proposal, with:
  The header: the freelancer's logo and name, the contract title, "Prepared for [Client]", the reference number.
  The sections (Parties, Scope, Payment, Timeline, Revisions, Cancellation, Liability, Signatures).
  The three actions at the bottom.
  The footer: "This contract is not a legally binding e-signature until both parties sign. The Approve action is a confirmation, not a signature." + the "Powered by Revercue" (removed on Scale/Teams).

The states:
  The password wall (if protected).
  The closed page (if archived).
  The expired banner (if past the expiry date).
  The invalid link.
  The approved state: "Approved. Thank you." + the after-approval next step (form/invoice/PDF).
  The declined state: "Declined. Thank you for letting us know."
  The question state: the text box; after submit, "Your question has been sent. You can still approve or decline."

# ============================================================
# PART 6 — THE DESIGN RULES
# ============================================================

The same rules as the proposal builder, with:
  The contract has NO FAQs and NO embedded questions. The "I Have Questions" is at the document level, not per question.
  The signature block is the contract's closing. The client's Approve is the equivalent of a signature (stated honestly in the modal).
  The after-approval chain is the thread's continuation. The contract is not the end; it is the bridge to the form or the invoice.
  The gold rule: the "Create" button in the builder, the "Approve" button on the client view. One gold per screen.
  The decline is a ghost button with a red border, not a red filled button. It is a secondary action, not the primary.
